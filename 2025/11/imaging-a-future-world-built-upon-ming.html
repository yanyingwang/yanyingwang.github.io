I’m going to update some news about Ming Language in this post. Besides, please follow me and imaging a whole new future world built upon Ming and imply Ming’s philosophy to our world and see where could we go next in the future.

What have I done for my Ming Language project

I had improved this project a lot compared to last time. Now it is very easy for people to understand what I’m trying to do and how my original idea works in reality. I have a Rationale to briefly elaborate this idea and now, if we check out the documents, we would notice that all the Chineseoid characters that constitute the names of procedures are explainable and jumpable to be knowing the origins and meanings. The Chineseoid characters are usually splitable for constituing by small units. And those units are usually used as another procedure or concept in the programming language world. We can follow the chains to understand the meanings of every procedure. Once we reach the ultimate ending of one character, it is linked to the https://zi.tools/ website to see the original meaning.

What do I want to do next about this project

This project is still in the very beginning state. If I want it to be really workable, I need to dig into the racket programming language and maybe change the core code of it to adapt some features of my Ming language.

Another important thing I think is concerned with the Chineseoid characters. I need to find a way to automatically generate them. And also, given the fact that most names of programming languages are written in English nowadays, I need to find a way to merge English with the characters.

Which means, firstly, I need to change the written system of English. Based on how English words are formed, I will break them down into prefix, suffix, and root. And then, I will shrink the length of each word and rearrange it both horizontally and vertically. There are a few rules I preliminarily summed up below:

  1. Join the prefix, suffix, and root as a part of the character.
  2. Omit the duplicated letters when they are adjacent.
  3. Rotate the letter by some degrees to efficiently use the space.

Let me demonstrate these rules by examples:

  1. define, give.
    As the picture shows, “D” and “E” from “define” are joined together in the left part of the first character. “FI” in “DEFINE” and “I” in “Give” are rotated 90 degrees to use the space efficiently.
  2. post.
    As the picture shows, there are two ways to write the word “POST”.
  3. keep.
    People might be wondering, this way of writing English does have a point, but it is hard to read, especially for native English speakers. You are just thinking in the Chinese way of writing. Actually, the reason to change the writing way of English has made us be possible to merge English characters and Chinese characters as a brand new character. Take the next example:
    From the picture above, it is a combination of English and Chinese to write “helper” and “keeper”. The “亻” character in Chinese means “human” or “people”, etc. It is used to constitute characters like 你 他 们, etc. I especially use it to replace “er” in “keeper” and “helper”.

By the way of changing the writing system of English and combining English words with Chinese, we can make a new natural language. And we will use this language for daily programming. Furthermore, we will obey the rules I already summed up in the Ming Language and extend them, and we may possibly make the editor suggest the input type and output type of a procedure as well as automatically suggest procedure names while programmers are writing the content of procedures. Because the procedures and syntaxes that the programmers are using are already named by following the rules. In this way, we could make the whole code world explainable and derivable.

Where would this project go next

Since the code world is explainable and derivable because of following the rules to give names to procedures and syntaxes, it could be possible to make the Semantic Web, at least I think the derivable and explainable names can be used for reasoning engines.

Another thing I can come up with is related to the decentralized network and communication. Because of the way of changing and combining English and Chinese, it will obviously create tons of new characters, and once people get used to this way, they will continuously create and create, the traditional Unicode system will not be sufficient. I guess the Unicode will still be workable for most time, but as of the daily communication, people may use an extended Unicode to communicate. And because of this unique way of using characters, even though the intermediate server can still intercept messages, it will be hard for it to understand them because they don’t know the details of the custom Unicode that the commentators are using.
